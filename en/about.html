About us

We teach and practice mounted archery in Argentina, for sporting, cultural and personal development purposes.

Who we are

Rider and horse, a harmonious bond

At Jinetes de Orión, we are dedicated to teaching and practicing mounted archery in Argentina. Our purpose is to value this activity and spread mounted archery for sporting, cultural, and personal development purposes.

We seek the creation of a harmonious bond between rider and horse, always prioritizing animal welfare, conscious horsemanship, and the technical precision of shooting both on foot and on the track. We propose an integral training space where discipline, mental focus, enjoyment, and respect for nature meet.

What guides us

Mission and vision

Mission

To train mounted archers in a safe and progressive manner, providing the technical tools and equestrian knowledge necessary for each student to develop their maximum potential, whether for recreational or competitive purposes.

Vision

To consolidate a reference center for mounted archery in the region to drive its growth at a national and international level. To promote a passionate community that shares the values of camaraderie, self-improvement, and respect.
