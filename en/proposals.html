Experiences

Proposals

Beyond regular lessons, we design gatherings with specific goals: sporting, personal and cultural.

Sports clinic

Two or three intensive days for those who want to get started in mounted archery from a technical and sporting perspective.

Designed so that, regardless of your current level, you can build the necessary foundations to start training with a purpose.

Personal development

We complement the work of equine therapists, psychologists, and ontological coaches by providing this activity for personal development purposes.

That is how Khiimori was born, a gathering where the horse as a reflection of our presence and the bow as a projection of our will become the great teachers of the journey.

Cultural experience

In a unique natural environment such as the one offered by the Patagonian forest, we develop the ideal proposal for those looking to try a different activity.

Here, the focus is placed not only on practice but also on the historical and cultural journey of archery and mounted archery.
