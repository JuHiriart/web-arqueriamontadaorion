The discipline

A tradition over 5,000 years old

Records have been found dating the origins of mounted archery back more than 5,000 years. It was in the Eurasian steppes where the communion between the bow, the human being, and the horse took place. The development of this combined skill allowed the expansion of peoples like never before and constituted a fundamental tool for survival for many centuries.

Today, those traditions common to so many cultures around the world have been recovered, and it is possible to practice mounted archery at a competitive level in more than 50 countries. At Jinetes de Orión, we offer lessons adapted to your level so you can achieve your goal while enjoying every stage of the journey.
